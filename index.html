Adventure Works — Executive Analytics Suite

An end-to-end BI solution for a bike manufacturer: $24.9M in revenue, 25,164 orders, 17,416 customers, across Jan 2020 – Jun 2022.

Problem

Leadership had raw sales and returns data but no way to see performance, spot what was driving revenue, or test a pricing decision before committing to it.

Approach

Built a star schema over two fact tables and seven dimensions, then wrote 42 DAX measures grouped by purpose — time intelligence, dynamic targets, customer drill-through, and a what-if price simulator.

Impact

Three interactive reports where targets recalculate themselves each month, and a slider re-forecasts revenue and profit in real time as price changes — no manual rebuild required.

Restaurant Order Analysis

A quarter of order data, queried to find what the menu was actually doing.

Problem

A restaurant had a full quarter of order history sitting in a database and no read on which menu items earned their place or who the highest-value customers were.

Approach

Joined order and menu tables in MySQL, then worked through menu performance, order volume by time period, and spend ranked by customer.

Impact

A clear view of top and bottom performing items, when demand actually peaks, and which customers drive a disproportionate share of revenue.

NYC Traffic Safety Analysis

238,000+ NYPD collision records, worked into a picture of when and why crashes happen.

Problem

Two years of raw collision reports across New York City, with no visibility into seasonal patterns or which contributing factors actually cause the most harm.

Approach

Cleaned and pivoted the dataset in Excel, then built a monthly trend line, a day-by-hour heatmap, and a ranked breakdown of contributing factors by both volume and severity.

Impact

Found that collisions peak May–October and cluster in the weekday evening rush — and that the most frequent cause isn't the most lethal one, which changes where you'd intervene.
