Robinhood — Marketing Channel Performance

Six months of ad data, ~$125K across six campaigns, read for which channels actually earned their spend and where the next budget should go.

$125K
Ad budget audited
1.9×
Top-channel ROAS
18,840
Signups analyzed

Problem

No read on what the ads produced. Robinhood spent ~$125K over six months with no view of which campaigns drove paying customers, not just cheap signups.

Approach

Joined $125K of spend to 18,840 signups in Athena. Cost per signup, cost per paying customer, and 90-day ROAS per campaign, then benchmarked paid against free.

Impact

The cheapest signups weren't the cheapest customers. Video drew $5 signups but only 3% paid, $159 each, so I shifted budget to retargeting and brand search.

Adventure Works — Executive Analytics Suite

An end-to-end BI solution for a bike manufacturer: $24.9M in revenue, 25,164 orders, 17,416 customers, across Jan 2020 – Jun 2022.

Problem

Raw data, no way to act on it. Leadership could see sales and returns but couldn't spot what drove revenue or test a pricing move first.

Approach

Star schema plus 42 DAX measures. Two fact tables and seven dimensions, with time intelligence, dynamic targets, drill-through, and a what-if price simulator.

Reports that update themselves. Targets recalculate monthly and a slider re-forecasts revenue and profit in real time as price changes.

NYC Traffic Safety Analysis

238,000+ NYPD collision records, worked into a picture of when and why crashes happen.

238K+
Collision records
2 yrs
Cleaned in Excel
May–Oct
Peak crash window

Problem

Two years of crashes, no pattern. Raw NYPD collision reports with no view of seasonal trends or which factors cause the most harm.

Approach

Cleaned and pivoted 238K records in Excel. Built a monthly trend line, a day-by-hour heatmap, and a ranked breakdown of contributing factors.

Impact

Frequent isn't the same as lethal. Crashes peak May to October in the weekday evening rush, and the most common cause isn't the deadliest, which changes where to intervene.
